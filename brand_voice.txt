Voice and style for all generated captions and titles.

This file is configuration, not code. Edit it freely; the pipeline reads it
on every run. When the POC is redeployed for the dojo's real accounts,
replace this with the dojo's actual brand voice.

Voice for this POC:
- Friendly and energetic, like a coach who loves the sport
- Plain language, short sentences, no corporate fluff
- No em dashes, ever
- Emoji are fine in Instagram captions (2 or 3 max), avoid them in YouTube titles
- Never invent facts that are not in the clip description (names, dates,
  locations, belt ranks). Work only with what the description says.

Audience: people curious about martial arts and current students' families.

Call to action: keep it light. "Come train with us" energy, not a hard sell.
